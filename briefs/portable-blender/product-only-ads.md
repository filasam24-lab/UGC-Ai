## Voiceover scripts

**A — 5s (12 words)**
> One cup. Blend it, drink it, rinse it. Yellow cart's right there.

**B — 8s (19 words)**
> Frozen fruit, splash of milk, press the top. Blends in the cup I drink from.
> Yellow cart's right there.

**C — 10s (20 words)**
> Charges off my phone cord. Fruit in, press, done. If you've dealt with this too,
> the yellow cart's right there.

Every line stays first-person and observational — no numeric, comparative, medical, or
performance claims. The CTA is the client's verbatim copy.

## Four-beat structure (each ad)

Each ad is one clip containing four internal hard cuts:
PRODUCT-INTRO → PRODUCT-DEMO-A → PRODUCT-DEMO-B → PRODUCT-RESULT.

- **A:** blender on counter → fruit dropped in (POV) → macro blend vortex → lifted away full,
  empty sink behind it
- **B:** lid off on counter → milk poured in (two hands) → thumb on the cap button (POV) →
  drinking from the spout, chin only
- **C:** charging on the counter → unplugged and lifted, nothing trailing → macro blend →
  capped and packed beside a tote bag

## Production notes

- Model: `seedance_2_0_mini` at 720p. `seedance_2_0` (1080p) is gated to Pro/Ultimate
  plans and returns 403 on a starter plan — the cost preflight does not check plan access,
  so this only surfaces at submission.
- Storyboards were rendered with `gpt_image_2` and cleaned with `seedream_v5_pro`. They are
  model-agnostic and can be re-fed to a higher-tier video model without regenerating.
- The product's real design comes from the TikTok Shop hero image attached as a reference to
  every generation, which overrides the written description where the two disagree.
