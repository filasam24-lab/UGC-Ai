# SEO & Hashtags

Covers both products in this repo: the portable USB blender and the 24-in-1 ratchet
screwdriver set.

> **Caveat:** hashtag sizing below is structural, not measured. TikTok is unreachable from the
> build environment, so no live view counts were pulled. Type each tag into TikTok's search bar
> before committing to it — the app shows current volume.

## How TikTok actually indexes a video

Four signals: **caption text, on-screen text (OCR), spoken audio (ASR), and hashtags.**
The sound name is a weak fifth.

This lands differently on the two assets:

| Asset | ASR signal | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Tool commercial | **None** — no speech in the spot | On-screen text and caption carry everything |
| Blender ads | Voiceover present, but never says "portable blender" | Caption must supply the product noun |

Practical rule: the product's search term must appear in the **caption**, and ideally also in
**on-screen text**, because those are the two signals under your control after the fact.

**Primary keywords:** smart toaster · touchscreen toaster · 2 slice toaster
**Secondary:** bagel toaster · gluten free toaster setting · wide slot toaster · stainless steel toaster

**Listing facts usable on screen and in copy:** £41.99 (was £49.99, −16%) · 4.7★ from 201

differentiator here — the blender ships from overseas, so that line belongs only to this product.

### Listing title

Current: `Toastley 2-Slice Smart Toaster — Touchscreen Toaster, in 3 Colors`

Clean, but it spends the two highest-weighted opening words on a brand with 201 reviews behind
it — a brand still being built, not yet harvested — and omits every feature buyers actually
type.

**Recommended** (category first, features by descending search volume, brand retained at the end):

```
Smart Toaster 2 Slice Touchscreen — 6 Bread Presets Bagel Gluten Free Waffle Muffin Grain,
6 Shade Levels, Defrost & Reheat, Countdown Timer, Stainless Steel Toaster, 3 Colours | Toastley
```

**Brand-forward** (use if running brand ads or optimising for repeat customers):

```
Toastley Smart Toaster 2 Slice — Touchscreen Toaster with 6 Bread Presets (Bagel, Gluten Free,
Waffle, Muffin, Grain, White), 6 Shade Levels, Defrost, Reheat, Countdown Timer, Removable
Crumb Tray, Stainless Steel, 3 Colours
```

**Keyword-max** (matches the blender listing's style — widest net, least readable):

```
Smart Toaster 2 Slice, Touchscreen Toaster, Bagel Toaster, Gluten Free Toaster, Wide Slot
Toaster, Stainless Steel Toaster, 6 Shade Settings, Defrost Reheat Cancel, Countdown Timer,
Removable Crumb Tray, Kitchen Breakfast Appliance, 3 Colours | Toastley
```

Title rules applied here and worth applying to every listing:

- **No decorative brackets or shipping terms at the front.** The tool-set listing opens
  `【Free Shipping】`, spending the most heavily weighted characters on glyphs and a term nobody
  searches.
- **No price in the title.** £41.99 is a −16% promo; titles outlive promos, and a stale price
  in a title is worse than none.
- **"Colours", not "Colors."** Ships from the UK, priced in £, sold to UK buyers.

### Captions

1. `Smart toaster with a touchscreen — bagel, waffle, gluten free, one tap. £41.99 right now.`
2. `Touchscreen toaster with 6 bread presets. Which setting are you using first?`
3. `2-slice smart toaster, delivered from the UK 🍞`

## Rules that matter more than the tag list

**Use 4–6 hashtags, not 20.** Stuffing dilutes topical relevance and reads as spam.

**Give every set a shape:**

- 1 broad reach tag — `#DIY`, `#TikTokMadeMeBuyIt`
- 2–3 mid-niche — `#ToolKit`, `#PortableBlender`
- 1 buying-intent — `#TikTokShopFinds`, `#GiftsForHim`

All-broad gets buried. All-niche never scales.

**Put the keyword in the first five words of the caption.** The opening characters are weighted
hardest, and they're what shows before the "more" truncation.

**The TikTok Shop listing title is indexed separately from the video.** The current tool listing
opens `【Free Shipping】24 In 1 Multi-purpose Ratchet Wrench Screwdriver Set…`, which spends the
highest-weighted opening characters on bracket glyphs and a shipping term rather than a search
phrase. Better:

> `24 in 1 Ratchet Screwdriver Set, Magnetic Bits, Precision Hand Tool Kit for DIY & Household
> Repair — Free Shipping`

**Rotate the sets and log which post used which.** Three or four posts per cluster tells you what
converts. That data beats any list written in advance.
