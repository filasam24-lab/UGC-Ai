rains in, splash crown, blend vortex through the front window, seamless loop. No people, no

### Claims policy

On-screen copy carries only what the listing states: price, discount, rating, units sold,
returns. No performance, health, capacity, or battery-life claims.

**`FREE RETURNS`, not `FREE SHIPPING`.** The tool-set listing offers free shipping; this one
offers free returns and ships from overseas. The line was not carried across between spots —
copying it would have advertised a shipping term this seller does not offer.

| 13.5–15s | Pull back and orbit to the exact opening framing | `FREE RETURNS` / `TAP THE YELLOW CART` |

The splash treatment is the literal Coca-Cola reference from the original brief. It was dropped
for the steel tool set — wet metal reads as rust — but this is a drink product, so it fits.

## Type sizing and safe area

Every line is measured before burning and capped at **≤560px wide in a 720px frame**. Two
reasons:

1. `PORTABLE BLENDER` at 74px measured **789px — wider than the frame**, and would have been
   clipped at both ends.
2. TikTok's right-hand button column overlays roughly the last 110px of width. Centred text
   wider than ~560px slides under the like/share/cart buttons.

The price sits at 80px (615px wide at 92px was too close to that column).
